Verdant

A quiet game about keeping houseplants alive, and a home worth keeping them in.

Verdant is a cozy botanical life sim for iPhone. You water, trim, repot and arrange a collection of real plants — ferns, monsteras, orchids, roses — across a home that grows from a studio apartment to a botanical estate.

It is in beta testing with family and friends. There is no release date yet.
