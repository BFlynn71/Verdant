Support

Something broken, or a plant behaving oddly? Here is what helps.

Reporting a problem

If you are testing through TestFlight, the quickest route is a screenshot: take one inside the app, then tap Share → TestFlight Feedback. It sends the picture along with your device and app version, which saves a lot of guessing.

Otherwise, email EMAIL-GOES-HERE with what you were doing, what happened, and what you expected instead.

Common questions

My plants moved after an update. Updates sometimes rebuild the rooms. The game puts every plant back in the room it was in, as close as it can to where it stood, but if a room itself changed, a plant may end up somewhere new. Nothing is ever lost.

A plant is wilting and I keep watering it. Check the light. A plant in the wrong light will struggle however well you water it; tap it to see what it wants, then carry it somewhere brighter or shadier.

A plant I want is greyed out in the shop. It needs a bigger home first. The card tells you which one. Some plants are also stocked only on some days.

My beta expired. TestFlight builds stop working 90 days after they are uploaded. Install the newest build and your garden carries on where it was.
