Privacy

Verdant collects nothing.

Last updated 13 November 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Verdant has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software kits of any kind. It does not ask for your contacts, your location, your photos or your health data, and it has no way to reach the internet.

Where your game is kept

Your plants, your coins and your progress are written to a single file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. That file never leaves the device. Nobody, including Bryan Flynn, can see it.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything goes with it. There is no copy anywhere else to ask about.

While the app is in beta testing

During beta testing, Verdant is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. TestFlight is Apple's service, not this app, and it collects information of its own: when you test a beta app, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. That data may include your name and email address, and testers cannot opt out of it. If you send feedback or a screenshot from TestFlight, that is shared with the developer too.

This is worth knowing before you install a beta of anything. Apple explains it in full at TestFlight & Privacy. Crash reports are used to fix crashes and nothing else.

Children

Verdant is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes — if a future version adds iCloud sync, say — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change with it.
